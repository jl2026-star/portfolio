Who · What · Why

There's the cut. And there's the system that keeps the cut findable. Most people pick one. I do both — and as far as I'm concerned, that's the whole job.

Four years at Comcast NBCUniversal. I lead creative on the company's TEDx-style speaker series, edit 200+ pieces a year, and produce social content across 20+ partner channels — 597K+ impressions, 15K+ monthly views. On stage and on camera: Barack Obama, Simone Biles, Jalen Hurts, Bianca Belair. On the back end: 1M+ assets across Iconik and LucidLink, plus the AI-tagged metadata framework I built that cut team search time by 20%.

Before Philly: cut Kourtney Kardashian's Poosh (3.3M+ views), TLC, Big Lots, Saktii, CreatorsTV.

What I care about: pacing in the cut, restraint in the type, and the unglamorous work that lets a creative team move fast — and find what they made two years ago.

Ohio State · B.S. Moving Image Production · Dean's List · Charles Newirth Scholar.
