06 / Bio
Who · What · Why

Jordan Lane is a producer in Philadelphia.

He likes making things — screenings, series, brand films, social cuts, live events — and he likes the quieter work of making the making itself better. The systems that route media, the taxonomies that make an archive searchable, the AI workflows that turn a decade of footage into a resource instead of a graveyard.

Most producers do one or the other. Jordan does both, and thinks of them as the same job: the show that goes out on Tuesday is inseparable from the pipeline that got it there.

Currently at Comcast NBCUniversal. Previously with Kourtney Kardashian's Poosh, Saktii, TLC, Big Lots, and CreatorsTV.
